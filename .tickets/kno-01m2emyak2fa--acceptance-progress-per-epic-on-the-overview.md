## Description
The parents table shows met over total criteria per epic as a number. Where the backlog actually stands, and which epic is nearest to done, is a comparison the reader has to make in their head across rows.

## Design
A progress bar per parent on the overview, sorted by remaining criteria ascending, with the children's done count beside it, so the two kinds of progress an epic has are visible together.
