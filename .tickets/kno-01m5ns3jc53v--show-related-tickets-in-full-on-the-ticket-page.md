## Description

The ticket page's relation tabs (children, blocked by and blocking, linked, siblings) and the depends-on tree draw each related ticket with its full row, as every list does, plus one fact per tab: a status summary over children and siblings, whether each open blocker is ready or blocked itself, an only-blocker mark on blocking, and ready or blocked state with priority and assignee in the dependency tree. The overview's recently changed and closed cards gain type and assignee, and compare rows gain each side's status and the assignee.
